NexusFintech Pvt. Ltd.
Workplace Conduct Policy
Document ID: POL-CONDUCT-01
Version: 2.0
Effective Date: 2025-03-15
Category: Policy

1.0 Purpose
This policy defines expected standards of professional conduct for all NexusFintech employees.

2.0 Professional Behaviour
2.1 Employees must treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect at all times.
2.2 Harassment, discrimination or bullying of any kind is prohibited.

3.0 Anti-Bribery & Anti-Corruption
3.1 Employees must never offer, accept or solicit bribes in any form.
3.2 Gifts above INR 2,000 must be declared to Compliance.
3.3 Sales and Marketing teams must follow ethical selling rules.

4.0 Confidentiality
4.1 Customer data, business plans and internal communications are confidential.
4.2 Disclosure to third parties requires written approval from the reporting manager.

5.0 Handling Conduct Complaints
5.1 All complaints must be logged within 24 hours.
5.2 HR Executives and Branch Managers must handle complaints per SOP-HR-04.
5.3 Retaliation against complainants is strictly prohibited.

6.0 Disciplinary Process
6.1 Violations may result in verbal warning, written warning, suspension or termination.
6.2 All disciplinary actions must be documented in the employee file.

This policy supersedes version 1.3 dated 2024-05-01.
