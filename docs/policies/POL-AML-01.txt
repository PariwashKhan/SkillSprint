NexusFintech Pvt. Ltd.
Anti-Money Laundering & KYC Policy
Document ID: POL-AML-01
Version: 3.2
Effective Date: 2025-04-01
Category: Policy

1.0 Purpose
This policy defines NexusFintech's obligations under PMLA 2002 and RBI KYC Master Directions.

2.0 Employee Obligations
2.1 All Finance, Sales, and Branch roles must complete AML/KYC certification within Week 1.
2.2 Suspicious transactions must be reported to the MLRO within 24 hours.
2.3 Customer due diligence (CDD) is mandatory for transactions above INR 50,000.

3.0 Transaction Monitoring
3.1 Automated alerts must be reviewed within 4 business hours.
3.2 Structuring indicators must be escalated immediately.

4.0 Record Keeping
4.1 KYC records must be retained for 5 years post account closure.
4.2 Audit trails are mandatory for all AML case files.

This policy supersedes version 3.1 dated 2024-09-15.
