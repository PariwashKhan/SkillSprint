Version: 1.3
Effective Date: 2025-02-10

3.0 Performance Review Cycle
3.1 Reviews conducted quarterly.
3.2 1:1 meetings mandatory every month for Team Leaders.
3.3 360-degree feedback used for Team Leaders and Branch Managers.

4.0 Feedback Guidelines
4.1 Feedback must be specific, timely, and constructive.
4.2 Document all feedback in the HR portal.

5.0 Handling Conduct Complaints
5.1 HR Executive must log complaints within 24 hours.
5.2 Conduct Committee reviews within 7 days.
5.3 Outcome communicated in writing within 10 days.
