1.3 Approved Expenses
1.3.1 Travel: Economy class for domestic, Business for international.
1.3.2 Meals: Up to INR 1,500 per day.
1.3.3 Client entertainment: Prior manager approval required above INR 5,000.

2.0 Submission Process
2.1 Submit expense reports within 30 days.
2.2 Receipts are mandatory for all claims.
2.3 Use the Expense Form in the Finance portal.

3.0 Approval
3.1 Manager approval required for all claims.
3.2 Finance Associate must verify GL code and cost center.
3.3 Reimbursement within 7 working days of approval.
