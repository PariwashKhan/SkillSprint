Customer Complaint Resolution SOP
Document ID: SOP-CX-02
Version: 3.0
Effective Date: 2025-02-01
Category: SOP

3.0 Complaint Handling Workflow
3.1 All complaints must be logged in CRM within 15 minutes of receipt.
3.2 Acknowledge the customer within 2 business hours.
3.3 Categorize: Service / Billing / Product / Fraud.

4.0 Resolution Timelines
4.1 Service complaints: 24 business hours.
4.2 Billing complaints: 48 business hours.
4.3 Fraud complaints: immediate escalation to Security and Compliance.

5.0 Closure
5.1 Customer must confirm resolution in writing.
5.2 Complaint records retained for 3 years.
