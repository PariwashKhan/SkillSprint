1.5 Approved Sales Scripts
Only approved scripts from the Sales Playbook v4 may be used.
Off-script claims about returns are prohibited.

2.0 Lead Qualification
2.1 All leads must pass the BANT framework.
2.2 Consent must be captured before any marketing communication (see POL-DATA-01 §5.1).

3.0 Product Knowledge
3.1 Every Sales Executive must pass the Product Certification within Week 1.
3.2 Re-certification is annual.

4.0 Ethical Selling
4.1 No pressure tactics. No misrepresentation of fees.
4.2 Anti-bribery rules in POL-CONDUCT-01 §3.0 apply at all times.
