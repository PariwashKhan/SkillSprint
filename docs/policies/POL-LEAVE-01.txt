Leave Policy

Version: 2.3
Effective Date: 2025-01-01
Category: Policy

2.0 Leave Categories
2.1 Annual Leave: 24 days per calendar year, accrued monthly.
2.2 Sick Leave: 12 days per calendar year, requires medical certificate beyond 2 days.
2.3 Casual Leave: 6 days per calendar year, cannot be carried forward.
2.4 Maternity Leave: 26 weeks as per statutory requirements.
2.5 Paternity Leave: 10 working days.

3.0 Application Process
3.1 All leave must be applied via the HR portal at least 3 days in advance.
3.2 Emergency leave must be informed to the reporting manager within 4 hours.

4.0 Administration
4.1 HR Executives must process leave requests within 48 hours.
4.2 Leave without pay requires HR Head approval.

5.0 Carry Forward Rules
5.1 Maximum 10 days of annual leave can be carried forward.
5.2 Sick and casual leave do not carry forward.

This policy supersedes version 2.2 dated 2024-08-15.
