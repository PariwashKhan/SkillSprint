NexusFintech Pvt. Ltd.
Information Security Policy
Document ID: POL-INFOSEC-01
Version: 2.1
Effective Date: 2025-01-15
Category: Policy

1. Purpose
This policy establishes the information security requirements for all employees, contractors and systems of NexusFintech.

2.1 Information Security Awareness
All employees must complete Information Security Awareness training within the first day of joining and annually thereafter. Failure to complete this training is a disciplinary matter.

2.2 Password and Access Control
- Passwords must be at least 12 characters and changed every 90 days.
- Credentials must never be shared.
- Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all critical systems.

3.2 Secure Support Practices (for technical roles)
Support engineers must follow secure remote access procedures and never leave sessions unattended.

4.1 Data Tools Security
Analysts and data roles must use only approved tools and follow least-privilege access.

5.0 System Access Policy
Access is granted on a need-to-know basis. All access requests must be logged and approved by the reporting manager.

Violation of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.
