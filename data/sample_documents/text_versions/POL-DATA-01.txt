Data Privacy Policy
Document ID: POL-DATA-01
Version: 1.4
Effective Date: 2025-03-01
Category: Policy

3.1 General Data Protection
All employees must acknowledge this Data Privacy Policy on Day 1. Personal data of customers must be handled only for legitimate business purposes.

4.2 Financial Records
Finance team members must ensure financial data is encrypted at rest and in transit. Access logs are mandatory.

5.1 Consent Collection
Sales and Marketing teams must obtain explicit consent before using customer data for campaigns. Consent records must be stored for 5 years.

6.0 Ticket Handling (Support)
When handling support tickets, PII must be masked in screenshots and logs. Full PII is visible only to authorised personnel.

7.0 Data Anonymisation (Analytics)
Data Analysts must anonymise or pseudonymise datasets before sharing outside the data team.

This policy supersedes version 1.3 dated 2024-06-01.
