4.2 Escalation Procedure for Customer Support
When a customer issue cannot be resolved at L1 within 30 minutes, the agent must escalate to L2 using the Escalation Form in the CRM.

Steps:
1. Document all actions taken.
2. Select correct escalation category (Technical / Billing / Complaint / Fraud).
3. Notify the customer of the escalation and expected response time (4 business hours for L2).
4. For fraud or data breach suspicion, escalate immediately to Security team and Compliance.

This section is mandatory for all Customer Support Executives.
